# Backlink Targets - stockmarketroi.com

Goal: raise domain authority the legitimate way (relevant, independent, editorial links).
Context: GSC bottleneck is authority of a young domain (the "discovered, not indexed" backlog), not a technical bug. Fix = relevant links + topical authority, not volume of thin pages.

Priority order below is by ROI vs effort. Work top-down.

---

## TIER 2 - Listicle inclusion outreach (highest relevance)

These are existing "best free stock screener 2026" articles. Email the author, politely ask them to consider adding Stock Market ROI. Relevance is perfect (finance -> finance).

**Stock screener listicles (live targets):**
- NerdWallet - "7 Best Free Stock Screeners of 2026" - nerdwallet.com/investing/learn/best-stock-screeners (high authority, hard but worth one shot)
- Gainify - "9 Best Free Stock Screeners in 2026" - gainify.io/blog/best-free-stock-screener
- Will Kopec - "Best Finviz Alternatives 2026" - willkopec.com/blog/best-finviz-alternatives-2026 (indie blogger, high reply rate)
- ChartingLens - "10 Best Finviz Alternatives" - chartinglens.com/blog/best-finviz-alternatives
- Deepvue - "Top 12 Finviz Alternatives" - deepvue.com/compare/finviz-alternatives
- VT Markets - "9 Best Free Stock Screeners in 2026" - vtmarkets.com/en-eu/discover/9-best-free-stock-screeners-in-2026

**Approach:** indie blogs (Will Kopec, ChartingLens, Gainify) reply far more than NerdWallet. Start there. Note: some of these ARE competitors (they list their own tool #1); the aggregator-style ones are the better ask.

**Pitch template (listicle inclusion):**
> Subject: Free screener for your "best free stock screener" roundup
>
> Hi [name], really useful roundup on [topic]. I run Stock Market ROI (stockmarketroi.com), a free US stock screener with dividend and compound-interest calculators and no signup wall. If you ever refresh the list, it might be a fit for readers looking for a no-cost option. Either way, thanks for the write-up. - Ivan

Keep it short, genuine, no pressure. One follow-up max after ~10 days.

## TIER 4 - Linkable assets / data-study PR (most durable)

The Mag7 data study (blog id 100) is exactly this. Journalists link to original data. Repeat quarterly.

**Play:** publish a data study -> pitch it to finance journalists (via Qwoted/Featured "looking for data" requests, or direct email) -> earn editorial links.

**Study ideas you can already generate from your data:**
- "What $1,000 in each Magnificent 7 stock is worth today" (updated)
- "S&P 500 dividend yield by sector, 2026"
- "How often does the market actually close green? (X years of data)"
- "The best and worst months for the S&P 500, historically"

## TIER 5 - Community (relationship, not spam)

- Reddit: r/investing, r/dividends, r/stocks - answer with genuine value, link a resource only when it truly helps. Build karma/account age first.
- Quora: target the same evergreen questions your posts already answer (good dividend yield, P/E ratio, live off dividends, compound interest).
- Fintwit (X): share the data studies; tag relevant accounts.

## AVOID (Google link-spam targets)

- Paid link schemes / link farms / spun-content directories
- Reciprocal link exchanges at scale
- Blog-comment spam
